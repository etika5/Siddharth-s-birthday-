for you,
Happy 25th
Birthday

You make my days special just by being present in them. Yet, I am not there with you on your special day. To make up for it, I thought this is the perfect time to show you what you wanted — the poems I've written about you, everything my heart has felt.

I
I wished for an everlasting love A soulmate, my dove And I met him out of nowhere Ended up in his arms with no care We were bound together In a messy entangled fate Too afraid to lose one another Too afraid to even stay There was something that lingered between us Something that was strong and true Something we build over time With laughter, tears, and feelings pure I hope this is where my dream finds its place I hope this is where my story begins I wished for an everlasting love and I hope it is you who will remain
II
As I hold you close in my arms I steal a glance at your face Wondering if this is my last Not knowing if I'll get another chance You look so perfect So blissful and kind It breaks my heart to even think about This might be my last time So I stare at you all I can Capturing the strands of your hair in my mind Studying the way your breath rises and falls Tracing your every little line The innocence that shines as you close your eyes Your lashes casting a shadow on your face It feels so sacred watching you up so close The way you rest with such grace I can never get tired of it I can admire it for hours So I stare at you all I can As you fall asleep in my arms
III
I still remember the night When you rested your head on my lap for the first time How I melted at that sight How you looked so vulnerable and…mine You warned me about touching your hair But it was too tempting the way they looked so soft How could I resist myself there When you were laying on me with such comfort? There was a warm feeling building inside me Something I've never felt before The way you talked made me feel so close to you And my heart took it for something more Then you take my hand in yours and say “Please stay for the night, please don't go” I still remember how I wished I could How difficult it was for me to say no But trust me darling if today You hold my hand asking me to stay I will leave everything behind And run into your arms to be by your side
IV
Maybe it is your tousled brown hair Minimal wardrobe and strong arms Maybe it's the way you smoothly talk Holding conversations with anyone for hours Maybe it is the way you twirl the pen between your fingers Or the way you shuffle the cards Or the way you play the piano With your entire body, giving it your all Maybe it is the way you calculate the sleep you'd get every night And the way you get it wrong every single time Maybe it is your voice captivating my mind Or the small acts of courtesy you leave behind Maybe it's the way your hair falls on your face Or how you're so cute and stubborn Maybe it's your full-teethed laughter Or how you look so hot in my favourite colour Maybe it is your teasing smile Your mocking tone and deep brown eyes Maybe it's your carefree attitude and sharp mind And the way you choose bitter truths over sweet lies Maybe it's your elegant hands, and the way they hold mine Or maybe it's the way you lock in when it's time Your sculpted body and pretty face Or maybe it's your tight warm embrace Maybe it's your humor and talent Or the way you eat to your heart's content Maybe it's the way you crave sweet Or maybe I just like the way you breathe
V
In a world full of lies He was looking for his truth He was one of a kind Wanting to get inside others' mind He desired for something whole A real feeling that'll stay No fancy promises of forever Just a routine warm “are you okay and how was your day?” His dreams were mundane But his aspirations high And he cared so so deeply About the people in his life He was smart and quick Yet he somehow believed That he could hide his tears With laughter and funny stories He was a man He has been through that heat But he was also a child The naughtiest you'll ever meet He was a star Shining the brightest amongst infinity He was a dream Turning into my reality
